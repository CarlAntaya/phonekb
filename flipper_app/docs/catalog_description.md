# Phone Keyboard

Use your Android phone as a **wireless keyboard and touchpad** for any computer. The Flipper receives input from your phone over Bluetooth and types it into the computer over USB, as a normal USB keyboard and mouse.

The computer needs no drivers, software or Bluetooth, so it also works in BIOS setup, on locked-down PCs, Raspberry Pis and smart TVs.

## How to use

1. Plug the Flipper into the computer with a USB cable and open **Phone Keyboard**.
2. On your Android phone, open the controller page in Chrome: https://carlantaya.github.io/phonekb/
3. Tap **Connect**, choose **PhoneKB**, and enter the 6-digit PIN shown on the Flipper.

On the Flipper: **Back** exits, **hold Left** forgets the paired phone.

## Phone controller features

- Touchpad with tap to click, two-finger right click and scroll, drag and hold click
- Type with your normal phone keyboard, including autocorrect and swipe typing
- Ctrl, Alt, Win and Shift, arrows, function keys, media keys and common shortcuts
- Macro recorder and editor, with a Remote screen of one-tap macro buttons
- Import and export macros between phones
- Works offline once installed from Chrome

## Requirements

- An Android phone with Chrome (iPhone browsers do not support Web Bluetooth)
- A USB cable from the Flipper to the computer
- The computer should use the US keyboard layout

The Bluetooth link is PIN-paired and encrypted. While the app is running, qFlipper and the Flipper mobile app cannot connect to the Flipper.
